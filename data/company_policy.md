# BIA Internal Policy Pack

## Domestic Travel Reimbursement

Employees travelling within India for approved business work may claim up to ₹3,000 per day for meals and local transport combined. Hotel expenses are reimbursed separately based on city tier and must include a tax invoice. Claims must be submitted within 10 working days of trip completion.

## International Travel Approval

International travel must be approved by the reporting manager and the finance approver before tickets are booked. Economy class is the default travel class. Exceptions require written approval from a department head.

## Expense Claim Documentation

Every expense claim must include the invoice, business purpose, date, vendor name, and payment proof. Claims above ₹5,000 require manager approval even when the expense category is normally pre-approved.

## Remote Work Security

Remote work is allowed for approved roles, but company data must be accessed only through the corporate VPN. Confidential files must not be downloaded to personal devices. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Laptop Replacement Policy

Standard laptop replacement is considered after 36 months of active use. Earlier replacement is possible when IT confirms hardware failure, battery health below acceptable range, or device incompatibility with required software.
